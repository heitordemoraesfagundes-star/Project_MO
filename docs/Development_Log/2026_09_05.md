# Development Log — 2026-09-05

## Session Objective

Continue verifying the remaining V1 components and reduce the prototype to only the parts required for basic functionality, safety, programming, and assembly.

## Work Completed

### V1 scope simplified

The internal JST connector system was removed from the minimum V1 requirements. Although detachable connectors would improve organization and maintenance, they are not necessary for the first functional prototype.

V1 will use direct soldered connections protected with heat-shrink tubing. Internal connectors may be reconsidered for V2.

### Power supply and cable verified

The existing Xiaomi MDY-15-ET phone charger was inspected.

Verified specifications:

* Normal output: 5 V / 3 A
* Original Xiaomi USB-A to USB-C cable
* Correct connector for the YG003 USB-C input board
* No visible exposed conductors or severe connector damage

The charger and cable are suitable for V1. The YG003 output voltage must still be measured before the complete electronics are connected.

### Nextion programming method verified

The official NX8048P050-011R package contains:

* Nextion 5-inch display
* XH2.54 four-wire cable
* Power-supply test board

It does not include a USB-to-TTL programming adapter or microSD card. A microSD card was therefore selected as the simplest V1 programming method.

### MicroSD card verified

Selected candidate:

* SanDisk Ultra SDSQUNR-032G-GN3MA
* 32 GB microSDHC
* Class 10 / UHS-I U1
* Full-size SD adapter included
* Seller with strong rating and sales history

The card must be tested after arrival and formatted as FAT32 before transferring the compiled Nextion `.tft` file.

A USB card reader will only be required if the computer does not have a full-size SD slot.

### Heat-shrink kit verified

The selected 560-piece kit was approved.

Verified specifications:

* Flame-retardant polyolefin
* 2:1 contraction ratio
* Operating temperature: −55 to 125 °C
* Full contraction temperature: approximately 120 °C
* 45 mm tube lengths
* Required 2, 3 and 5 mm sizes included

Planned use:

* 2 mm for 26 AWG signal-wire joints
* 3 mm for individual 20–22 AWG joints
* 3.5–4 mm for larger terminals
* 5–6 mm for wire groups and strain relief

Any heat application and soldering will be handled with appropriate adult supervision and equipment.

### Perfboard verified

Selected board:

* Smart Kits single-sided perforated board
* FR4 fiberglass
* 100 × 150 mm
* 1.6 mm thickness
* 35 μm copper
* 37 × 55 holes
* 2.54 mm hole spacing
* 1.0 mm hole diameter
* HASL finish

The hole grid matches the KSA switch footprint:

* 7.62 mm spacing equals three 2.54 mm intervals
* 5.08 mm spacing equals two 2.54 mm intervals

### Micro-USB cable inspected

An existing cable was visually confirmed to use a Micro-USB connector compatible with the selected ESP32.

Its data-transfer capability has not yet been tested. It remains provisionally approved until it can be connected to the ESP32 and computer.

### Fastener arrangement clarified

The following mounting interfaces were reviewed:

#### Faceplate to body

* 3.4 mm clearance holes in the faceplate
* 2.6 mm pilot holes in the body
* Four M3 × 10 mm self-tapping/thread-forming screws planned

#### Display upper retainer

* 2.6 mm pilot holes
* Two M3 × 12 mm self-tapping/thread-forming screws planned

Pan-head screws intended for plastic are preferred. Small packs with spare screws should be purchased.

## Issue Discovered

The perfboard has 1.0 mm grid holes, while the planned mounting fasteners are approximately 1.6–2.0 mm in diameter. These screws cannot pass safely through the grid holes.

The current perfboard mounting system therefore requires a minor CAD revision. The preferred solution is to use printed edge-retaining tabs or clamps so no screws pass through the board.

This revision was intentionally postponed until the next CAD session.
