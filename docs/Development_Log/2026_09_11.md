# Project MO - Development Log

**Date:** September 11, 2026  
**Session focus:** Printable-solid verification, 3MF validation, and V1 manufacturing-release packaging

## Session objective

Convert the completed enclosure design into a clear and reproducible manufacturing package by standardizing part names, verifying every revised printable solid, validating the final 3MF exports in PrusaSlicer, preserving the editable Fusion assembly, and updating the production documentation.

  - Main Body
  - FacePlate
  - Display Upper Retainer
  - Four position-specific Perfboard Retainers
  - D-pad, large circular, small circular, and triangle buttons
  - D-pad Center Pivot
  - Left and right arms
  - Left and right legs

  - `Perfboard_Retainer_Lower_Left`

### 2. FacePlate and Main Body release checks

- Confirmed that the FacePlate contains one production body and no reference solid inside its Bodies folder.
- Rechecked the FacePlate interfaces:

## Decisions made

1. Treat **2.929 mm** as the final V1 perfboard-retainer overlap because it does not obstruct required solder pads.
2. Store each position-specific perfboard retainer as a separate 3MF file.
3. Keep one editable Fusion source document containing all four retainer components.
4. Use 3MF as the final production mesh format and retain the old STL files only as legacy backups.
5. Maintain 16 unique production files and print the D-pad Center Pivot three times for 18 total pieces.
6. Reuse the previously verified arm and leg exports because their geometry has not changed.
7. Preserve the complete linked Fusion assembly as an F3Z archive.
8. Keep cable-management additions outside the V1 manufacturing release.
