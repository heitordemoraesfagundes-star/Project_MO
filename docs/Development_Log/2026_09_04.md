# Development Log — Component Verification and Initial Procurement

**Date:** September 4, 2026
**Project:** Project MO — V1
**Focus:** Button assembly verification, BOM update, and component procurement

## Objectives

The objectives for today were to:

* Complete the mechanical verification of the tactile-switch and perfboard assembly.
* Verify the operation of the D-pad and three independent face buttons.
* Finalize the selected perfboard and tactile switches.
* Update the Project MO Bill of Materials.
* Purchase the components that have already passed supplier and CAD verification.

## Work Completed

### 1. Perfboard reference completed

A detailed CAD reference was created for the selected SmartKits single-sided FR-4 perfboard.

The original board measures 100 × 150 × 1.6 mm and uses a 2.54 mm hole pitch with 1.0 mm holes. The section required for Project MO contains 44 × 20 holes and measures 111.76 × 50.80 mm.

The outer hole centers were positioned 1.27 mm from every cut edge, maintaining the regular 2.54 mm grid across the entire board.

### 2. Tactile-switch reference created

A reference model was created for the selected C&K KSA0M911LFTR momentary tactile switch.

The switch reference was initially designed using:

* 7.4 × 7.4 mm body envelope
* Approximately 4.75 mm total height above the PCB
* 7.62 × 5.08 mm terminal-center pattern
* Through-hole mounting
* 300 gf nominal operating force
* SPST normally-open operation

After reviewing the manufacturer documentation, it was discovered that the exact switch uses two diagonally positioned electrical terminals rather than the four-terminal arrangement used in the initial simplified model. The switch centers and button positions remain valid because the two actual terminals still align with the 2.54 mm perfboard grid. The terminal geometry in the CAD reference and BOM notes must be corrected in the next revision.

### 3. Seven switches positioned

Seven switch references were positioned beneath the controls:

* Four switches for the D-pad directions
* One switch for the triangular button
* One switch for the small circular button
* One switch for the large circular button

Construction sketches were used to mark the actuator centers, making it easier to align each switch with its corresponding button.

### 4. PCB mounting height corrected

The first perfboard position caused approximately 1.50 mm of interference between the switches and buttons.

The four PCB mounting supports were extended by 1.80 mm, moving the perfboard farther from the faceplate. This replaced the interference with approximately 0.30 mm of resting clearance between the buttons and switch actuators.

The perfboard remained parallel to the faceplate after the adjustment.

### 5. Button operation verified

The three independent face buttons were checked individually. Each button can:

* Close the 0.30 mm resting gap
* Reach its intended switch
* Move without binding against the faceplate
* Return to its resting position
* Avoid interfering with neighboring switches

### 6. D-pad operation verified

The D-pad was tested by rotating it approximately 2–5 degrees in each direction.

The test confirmed that:

* Each direction reaches its intended switch.
* Adjacent switches are not contacted unintentionally.
* The D-pad remains clear of the faceplate during movement.
* The center pivot remains inside the center hole.
* The D-pad cannot move straight downward and activate multiple switches simultaneously.

Because lowering the PCB also lowered the D-pad pivot, the pivot height was extended until it once again reached the internal top of the D-pad’s center hole. This restored the intended fulcrum contact.

### 7. Internal clearance checked

The distance between the switch-pin ends and the interior rear surface of the enclosure was measured at approximately 51.50 mm.

This provides sufficient space for:

* Switch terminals
* Solder joints
* Internal wiring
* Wire bends and routing
* Future servicing

### 8. BOM updated

The Bill of Materials was updated to Revision 1.6.

The update added:

* C&K KSA0M911LFTR tactile switches
* Ten-switch purchase quantity
* Seven installed switches and three spares
* SmartKits FR-4 perfboard
* Final perfboard section dimensions
* Switch and button verification results
* D-pad pivot modification
* PCB support extension
* Supplier links and estimated prices

A future BOM revision must correct the preliminary four-terminal switch description to the manufacturer-confirmed two-terminal layout.

## Components Purchased

The following seven verified component groups were ordered:

1. DOIT ESP32 ESP-WROOM-32 DevKit V1
2. Nextion Intelligent 5-inch NX8048P050-011R display
3. 40CCG08HX 40 mm, 8 Ω, 3 W speaker
4. AITRIP MAX98357A I²S amplifier
5. Ten C&K KSA0M911LFTR tactile switches
6. Geekworm/eleUniverse YG003 USB-C input board
7. SmartKits FR-4 perfboard

Seven switches will be installed in the robot, leaving three spare switches for replacement or testing.

## Final Result

The complete button and perfboard assembly is now provisionally mechanically verified in CAD. The selected electronic components have also entered the procurement stage, marking the beginning of Project MO’s physical-build phase.

Final tolerances will be confirmed using the real components before ordering the final enclosure print.
