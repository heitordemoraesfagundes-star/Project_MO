* Measured the D-pad center socket as approximately 2.4 × 2.4 mm with a depth of 2.2 mm

  * Ø2.0 mm stem
  * 7.5 mm total height
  * 0.8 mm rounded top fillet

* Verified perfboard screw access and completed the full assembly/serviceability review
* Saved the checkpoint `Goal 6A – Assembly and Serviceability Review Complete`

## Decisions Made

* The speaker, amplifier, ESP32, display, and display retainer will use a deliberate service order rather than being treated as simultaneously accessible
* The display must be removable without disturbing the FacePlate button subassembly
* The perfboard connection will use a detachable inward-facing connector with a flexible service loop
* Dedicated internal cable clips will be deferred to V2 unless physical assembly testing shows that they are necessary
* The D-pad requires a separate center pivot attached to the perfboard
* The D-pad pivot will use a wide adhesive flange so the narrow stem remains stable during repeated use
* Goal 5 is considered complete following the internal-mount, ventilation, connector-access, and interference checks

## Problems/Blockers

* The D-pad did not include a suitable central support in the original button geometry
* The pivot height had to account for both the 2.2 mm socket depth and the measured 5.3 mm gap to the perfboard
* Internal serviceability requires the display to be removed before accessing the speaker and amplifier
* Exact connector dimensions and the final service-loop length must still be verified using the purchased hardware
* The electronic reference models remain provisional and must be checked against the physical components before production printing
