## Decisions Made

* PETG will be requested for the complete V1 printed-parts quote because it provides better toughness and heat resistance than PLA for snap clips, screw bosses, and limb pegs
* The final production package will use 3MF mesh files rather than client-generated G-code
* The original buttons will retain their existing mesh geometry and will not be redundantly re-exported from Fusion
* Left and right arm and leg parts will remain separate replacement files
* The main body requires support only beneath the speaker cradle according to the slicer validation; the display ledge was accepted as printable without generated support
* Arms require support because of their curved undersides, while legs require both support and a brim because of limited bed contact
* Three D-pad center pivots will be ordered because the part is small and easy to lose
* The full enclosure should not be ordered until the exact purchased electronics have been verified

## Problems/Blockers

* The initial default PrusaSlicer printer profile was too small for the approximately 150 × 200 × 60 mm enclosure and produced an out-of-bounds warning
* The temporary XL 5T profile generated a wipe tower that had to be disabled because only single-material geometry inspection was required
* The arm and leg meshes retained their Fusion assembly orientations and required reorientation in PrusaSlicer
* Both arms produced floating-feature warnings without support
* Both legs produced floating-bridge, loose-extrusion, and low-bed-adhesion warnings without support and a brim
* Exact dimensions of the purchased display, ESP32, amplifier, speaker, perfboard, and connectors remain unverified
* A professional 3D-printing supplier and final printing price have not yet been selected
