# Project MO — Development Log

**Date:** 2026-08-26  
**Session:** Front-Panel Completion & Initial Seated-Leg Design

## Objectives

* Complete the remaining front-panel features for Goal 3
* Confirm the final assembled appearance of the faceplate
* Begin Goal 4 by defining and modeling simplified seated legs

## Work Completed

* Created a 60 × 8 mm horizontal slot beneath the display
* Cut the slot through the faceplate and assigned it as a passive ventilation intake
* Created two 18 × 8 mm rounded rectangular details near the lower-left corner
* Recessed both rectangular details by 1 mm instead of cutting unnecessary through-holes
* Deferred the microphone opening until the microphone module and mounting position are confirmed
* Updated the full assembly and visually verified the display, controls, slot, recesses, and screw holes
* Completed Goal 3: Front-Panel Layout and Openings
* Began Goal 4: External Parts
* Defined the legs as simplified, static parts that create a seated appearance without carrying the enclosure's weight
* Positioned the original leg meshes as visual references
* Created initial left and right seated-leg models with rounded front profiles
* Converted the leg bodies into separate components
* Reduced the main leg depth from 10 mm to 8 mm while retaining a 5 mm foot projection
* Confirmed a total leg depth of 13 mm as a suitable initial proportion

## Decisions Made

* The horizontal slot will remain a through-opening and serve as passive ventilation
* The two lower rectangles will remain decorative 1 mm recesses for V1
* No separate status-LED opening is required because the display can communicate system status
* The microphone opening will be added only after its exact hardware location is established
* The silicone rubber feet—not the decorative legs—will support the enclosure
* The new legs will preserve the original seated appearance while using simpler, printable geometry
* Current leg dimensions are `LegBaseDepth = 8 mm` and `FootProjection = 5 mm`, producing a 13 mm total depth
* Small modeled screw threads are unnecessary; the bosses will use smooth pilot holes for self-tapping screws

## Problems/Blockers

* The diagonal orientation of the original mesh legs made a single side-section plane unsuitable for tracing their shape
* The silicone-foot dimensions are not yet confirmed, so the final leg ground clearance and underside geometry remain pending
* The arm geometry and mounting interfaces have not yet been started

## Next Steps

* Refine one seated leg with rounded transitions and suitable fillets
* Design the removable leg mounting interface
* Confirm the silicone-foot height and maintain approximately 0.5 mm leg-to-table clearance
* Apply the finalized design symmetrically to the opposite leg
* Create the simplified static arms and their attachment points
* Run final assembly and printability checks for all external parts
