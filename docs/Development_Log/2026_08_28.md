# Project MO — Development Log

**Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Session:** Goal 5 — Display and ESP32 Internal Mounting

## Objectives

* Begin Goal 5 by designing the internal hardware-mounting system
* Secure the Nextion display independently from the removable faceplate
* Establish the ESP32's final position, rear USB access, and removable mounting interface

## Work Completed

* Created a lower display shelf joined to the main enclosure
* Added two rear display stops, forming an L-shaped lower support system
* Created upper display bosses with pilot holes at the display-clearance depth
* Designed a separate removable upper retaining bar with rounded corners and two screw-clearance holes
* Converted and renamed the retaining bar as `Display_Upper_Retainer`
* Verified 0.00 mm³ volumetric interference between the display clearance, retainer, and main body
* Created a simplified ESP32 reference model using a 51 × 28 × 1.6 mm PCB
* Created a 55 × 32 × 2.5 mm mounting-base reference with a 3 mm PCB air gap
* Added a simplified 8 × 6 × 3 mm USB connector reference
* Selected a rear-facing USB position after comparing rear and side access
* Created a rounded 10 × 5 mm USB-clearance body and cut the corresponding rear enclosure opening
* Joined the ESP32 mounting base to the main enclosure
* Added four Ø4 × 3 mm PCB supports positioned away from the header-pin rows
* Created two 10 mm side clips with 0.3 mm lateral clearance
* Added 0.8 mm retaining lips to prevent the ESP32 from lifting from its supports
* Completed interference and clearance checks for the ESP32 mount and USB opening

## Decisions Made

* The display will be supported by the main enclosure rather than by the faceplate
* The display uses a fixed lower shelf and stops plus a removable screw-mounted upper retainer
* The ESP32 USB connector will face the rear of the enclosure
* Rear USB access was selected because side access conflicted with the arms or produced an awkward port location
* The ESP32 does not need to remain close to the display; clean UART and power-wire routing will be sufficient
* The MAX98357A amplifier should remain comparatively close to the speaker
* The ESP32 will use integrated snap-style side clips rather than mounting screws
* The clip walls rise 4.9 mm from the base, followed by 0.8 mm retaining lips with 0.8 mm PCB overlap

## Problems/Blockers

* The initial upper-retainer dimensions interfered with the enclosure's curved internal corners
* R4 mm sketch fillets were added to the retainer's upper corners to preserve its strength while clearing the enclosure
* Projected geometry divided the retaining-bar sketch into several profiles, although they correctly merged into one continuous body when extruded
* The initial clip-height recommendation was revised so the retaining-lip undersides maintain approximately 0.3 mm clearance above the PCB
* Exact real-world ESP32 and USB dimensions must still be confirmed against the purchased board before printing
