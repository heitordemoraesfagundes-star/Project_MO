# Project MO — Development Log

**Date:** 2026-08-24
**Session:** Parametric Enclosure Design & Display Fit Verification

## Objectives

* Replace the unreliable mesh-based enclosure workflow with a simplified parametric design
* Freeze the principal V1 hardware and master enclosure dimensions
* Create the new main body and matching faceplate
* Model and verify the installation envelope of the selected display
* Complete the first goal of the revised six-goal development plan

## Work Completed

* Reassessed the strategy of preserving the original STL enclosure
* Determined that a new parametric enclosure would provide more reliable dimensions, editing, and collision checks
* Selected the Nextion Basic NX8048T050 as the V1 display after removing the dimensional limitations of the original enclosure
* Retained the ESP32, MAX98357A amplifier, 40 mm speaker, physical buttons, and D-pad for V1
* Created Fusion 360 master parameters for the new enclosure, including:

  * Body width: 150 mm
  * Body height: 200 mm
  * Body depth: 65 mm
  * Wall thickness: 3 mm
  * Faceplate thickness: 3 mm
  * Rear-wall thickness: 3 mm
  * Corner radius: 16 mm
  * General clearance: 2 mm
  * Preliminary screen opening: 106 mm × 63 mm
  * Calculated internal dimensions: 144 mm × 194 mm × 59 mm
* Created the new `MO_Main_Body` as a rounded parametric solid
* Corrected the fillet selection so only the four depth-direction corner edges received the 16 mm radius
* Shelled the main body inward to create 3 mm walls
* Defined the open side as the front of the enclosure and the closed side as the rear wall
* Created the `Face_Plate` as a separate editable Fusion 360 file
* Modeled the faceplate with:

  * External dimensions of 150 mm × 200 mm × 3 mm
  * A 16 mm external corner radius
  * A 3 mm-deep locating lip
  * A 3 mm lip width
  * A 0.5 mm fit clearance per side
  * Lip external dimensions of 143 mm × 193 mm
* Created the `MO_Full_Body` assembly
* Inserted and aligned the main body and faceplate
* Confirmed that the locating lip fits the main body without solid interference
* Created a simplified parametric Nextion display model containing:

  * Physical module: 133.5 mm × 84 mm × 6.2 mm
  * Visible area: 118.5 mm × 77.55 mm
  * Active area: 108 mm × 64.8 mm
  * Installation-clearance envelope: 136.5 mm × 89 mm × 12 mm
* Added visual references for the active and visible screen areas
* Created a constrained display-position reference point 42 mm above the faceplate center
* Attached the display to the inside of the faceplate using a rigid assembly joint
* Confirmed zero interference between the physical display and faceplate
* Reduced the clearance-envelope width from 138 mm to 136.5 mm to prevent unnecessary contact with the faceplate lip
* Measured only 0.005 mm³ of clearance-envelope interference with the faceplate, which was treated as negligible calculation tolerance
* Confirmed that the complete display-clearance envelope does not interfere with the main body
* Completed Goal 1 of the revised weekly plan: hardware and master-dimension verification
* Created DL-010: Replacement of the Original Mesh-Based Enclosure with a New Parametric Model

## Decisions Made

* The original STL enclosure will no longer serve as the production CAD foundation
* A new simplified parametric enclosure will be developed directly in Fusion 360
* The original model will remain a dimensional, visual, and stylistic reference
* DL-010 supersedes the enclosure strategy documented in DL-009
* The Nextion Basic NX8048T050 will be used as the V1 display reference
* The new enclosure will use external dimensions of 150 mm × 200 mm × 65 mm
* The enclosure will use a container-and-faceplate architecture with an open front and closed rear wall
* The faceplate will remain a separate linked file to simplify future modification
* A locating lip with 0.5 mm clearance per side will align the faceplate with the main body
* Display-clearance geometry will be treated as reference geometry and evaluated separately from the physical module
* Goal 1 is complete; Goal 2 remains in progress until the faceplate fastening system is added and verified

## Problems/Blockers

* The original mesh files remained difficult to convert, inspect, and modify reliably
* The first corner-radius parameter was accidentally entered as 2 mm and was corrected to 16 mm
* The initial fillet affected more edges than intended and was corrected to the four depth-direction corner edges
* The open side of the body was initially interpreted as the rear but was redefined as the front to preserve the original container-and-cover architecture
* The initial 138 mm display-clearance envelope overlapped the faceplate lip by 266.709 mm³
* Reducing the clearance width to 136.5 mm lowered the remaining result to a negligible 0.005 mm³
* The faceplate fastening method has not yet been modeled
