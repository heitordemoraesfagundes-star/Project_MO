# Project MO - Development Log

**Date:** September 10, 2026  
**Session focus:** Main power-switch integration and button-perfboard mounting finalization

## Session objective

Resolve the remaining mechanical uncertainty around the selected main power switch, finalize the mounting system for the button perfboard, update the component documentation, and complete the enclosure changes required before the final manufacturing review.

## Work completed

### 1. Main power-switch specification confirmation

- Reviewed the supplier's additional dimensional information for the selected round latching rocker switch.
- Confirmed the switch as a two-terminal ON/OFF device with advertised ratings covering **12-24 V DC**, **127-220 V AC**, and **10 A**.
- Recorded the supplier-confirmed dimensions:
  - Upper flange diameter: **22.9 mm**
  - Panel-body diameter: approximately **19.6 mm**
- Retained the switch as the leading Project MO V1 power-switch choice because its electrical rating provides substantial margin above the planned **5 V / 3 A** input.
- Kept total body depth and terminal projection as physical-verification items because these values were not fully confirmed by the supplier.

### 2. Power-switch opening and clearance model

- Added a **20.0 mm diameter** switch opening to the main enclosure.
- Created a provisional internal clearance cylinder measuring **19.6 mm in diameter and 30 mm deep**.
- Positioned the clearance reference through the intended panel location and checked it against nearby internal geometry.
- Ran an interference check and confirmed that the provisional switch envelope does not collide with the enclosure or adjacent features.
- Moved the nearby lower flange region upward by **1 mm** to improve separation.
- Confirmed approximately **3.05 mm** of remaining clearance in the revised area.
- Measured approximately **4.5 mm** of surrounding material near the switch cutout and lower flange region.

### 3. Button-perfboard support redesign

- Replaced the earlier direct perfboard-fastening approach with a two-part retention system:
  - Four fixed support bosses integrated into the FacePlate
  - Four removable upper retainers secured with screws
- Finalized the fixed support bosses with:
  - **7.0 mm outer diameter**
  - **1.6 mm pilot holes**
  - **4.5 mm pilot/support depth**
- Confirmed approximately **2.7 mm radial material** around each pilot hole.
- Preserved the support locations around the perfboard without obstructing required soldering areas.

### 4. Removable perfboard retainers

- Created four position-specific retainers for the upper-left, upper-right, lower-left, and lower-right mounting points.
- Changed the retainer screw-clearance holes to **2.2 mm** for M2 hardware.
- Selected **M2 x 8 mm self-tapping/thread-forming screws** for the revised system.
- Confirmed approximately **4.4 mm of thread engagement** in the printed FacePlate bosses.
- Added **R2.0 mm side fillets** to resolve contact between the retainers and nearby FacePlate or enclosure-boss geometry.
- Rechecked the revised geometry and found no unintended solid interference.

### 5. Assembly-level mechanical verification

- Rechecked the FacePlate, Main Body, display mounting, switch clearance, button-perfboard supports, and surrounding internal geometry.
- Confirmed that the revised perfboard mounts do not conflict with the enclosure-closing path.
- Confirmed that the FacePlate and Main Body remain compatible after the new switch opening and support features.
- Ran a complete assembly interference check and found **no unintended interference**.

### 6. BOM update

- Updated the Project MO V1 Components BOM to **Revision 1.11**.
- Updated switch entry `C-019` with:
  - Round latching rocker-switch description
  - Two-terminal configuration
  - Advertised voltage and current ratings
  - Supplier-confirmed 22.9 mm flange and approximately 19.6 mm body diameter
  - Quantity and recorded price
  - Supplier and Mercado Livre listing information
  - Mechanical-fit status and remaining depth-verification note

## Decisions made

1. Use a **20.0 mm panel opening** for the selected approximately 19.6 mm switch body.
2. Retain the selected round latching rocker switch for V1, subject to physical depth and terminal-clearance verification.
3. Use four fixed FacePlate bosses and four removable upper retainers for the button perfboard.
4. Standardize the support bosses at **7.0 mm outer diameter with 1.6 mm pilot holes**.
5. Use **M2 x 8 mm** screws through **2.2 mm retainer clearance holes**.
6. Add **R2.0 mm fillets** to the retainers where necessary to eliminate enclosure contact.
7. Keep cable clips, channels, and other cable-management additions outside the V1 scope.
