# Project MO — Development Log

**Date:** 2026-09-01

## Objectives

* Review the display mounting system in its intended vertical operating orientation.

## Work Completed

* Reviewed the display mounting system while accounting for the robot’s intended vertical orientation.

  * Confirmed that gravity will pull the display toward the robot’s feet rather than toward the inside of the enclosure.
  * Extended the lower support along the bottom edge of the display to increase its contact area without unnecessarily increasing its width.
  * Checked that the revised support would not interfere with the planned cable-routing area.

* Improved the display’s upper retaining system.

  * Identified that the original upper retainer primarily prevented vertical movement but provided insufficient resistance against inward movement.
  * Added rear-support extensions near both ends of the retainer.
  * Preserved the retainer’s original upper capture feature.
  * Increased the screw-path region to approximately 6 mm.
  * Confirmed alignment between the retainer holes and the relocated mounting bosses.
  * Checked the completed retainer for interference with the display and enclosure.

* Revised the mounting hardware specification.

  * Retained M3 × 10 mm self-tapping/thread-forming screws for the FacePlate.
  * Changed the display upper-retainer screws to M3 × 12 mm.
  * Retained M2 × 5 mm screws for the perfboard.
  * Added a requirement to confirm at least 6 mm of usable pilot depth before installing the M3 × 12 mm screws.
  * Noted that screws longer than 12 mm should not be used without checking for breakthrough.

* Completed the final PrusaSlicer review of the printed parts.

  * Confirmed PETG as the intended material.
  * Reviewed the main-body orientation and required build-plate supports.
  * Reviewed the revised display upper-retainer orientation.
  * Confirmed that the original button meshes trigger PrusaSlicer’s meter-to-millimeter correction.
  * Documented that the automatic correction should be accepted only once and that no second manual scaling operation should be applied.

* Updated the 3D-printing manufacturing documentation.

* Reviewed and revised the complete V1 BOM.

  * Updated the controller to the ESP32 DevKit V1, 30-pin reference.
  * Replaced the outdated SPI display entry with the Nextion NX8048P050-011R 5-inch HMI.
  * Standardized the amplifier selection as the MAX98357A I²S module.

  * Updated the USB-C power-input entry to show that the exact breakout board is still pending.
  * Clarified that the custom PCB is deferred to V2.
  * Marked the heat-set inserts as an optional alternative because the current V1 design uses self-tapping/thread-forming screws.

## Decisions Made

* The display supports must be evaluated according to the robot’s vertical operating orientation, even though the main body is printed horizontally.
* The upper display retainer will provide both vertical retention and rear support against inward movement.
* The display upper retainer will use M3 × 12 mm screws, subject to the pilot-depth check.
* The current V1 design will use self-tapping/thread-forming screws rather than heat-set inserts.
* The custom PCB will remain a V2 component and will not be included in the V1 purchasing total.
* The MF-R110 1.1 A resettable fuse will remain only a candidate until the system’s maximum current demand is calculated.
* The BOM will remain in Excel until the 3D-printing quotations and remaining component checks are complete. It will then receive one final update and be converted to Markdown for GitHub.

## Problems/Blockers

* The exact current requirement of the complete system has not yet been calculated.
* The final external 5 V supply is not yet listed in the BOM.
* The USB-C breakout board, power switch and resettable fuse cannot be finalized until the power budget is complete.
* The exact purchased ESP32 connector and board dimensions still require confirmation.
* The Nextion display’s rear connector and cable route must be checked against the physical unit.
* The speaker depth, rated power and terminal orientation remain to be confirmed.
* The MAX98357A module dimensions and terminal positions remain to be confirmed.
* The tactile-switch quantity and actuator dimensions depend on the final D-pad and face-button arrangement.
* The perfboard dimensions, hole pitch and mounting-hole positions still require confirmation.
* Final 3D-printing prices are pending supplier quotations.
