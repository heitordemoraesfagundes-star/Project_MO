# Project MO Development Log

**Date:** September 14, 2026  
**Project Phase:** V1 Manufacturing Preparation and Physical Validation

## Session Objectives

- Complete the dimensional fit-test coupon set.
- Prepare a physical test plan for validating the real components.
- Assemble a supplier-ready manufacturing package.
- Identify possible Brazilian 3D-printing suppliers.
- Begin the quotation process without generating printer-specific G-code.

## Work Completed

### Fit-Test Coupon Set

The complete set of eight fit-test coupon designs was reviewed and exported from Fusion 360:

- Fastener fit coupon
- Switch fit coupon
- Limb-interface fit coupon
- Dedicated USB-C fit coupon
- Microphone fit coupon
- ESP32 fit coupon
- Amplifier fit coupon
- Speaker fit coupon

The limb-interface file contains four separate printed pieces, bringing the total physical piece count to eleven. All eight 3MF containers passed archive-integrity checks and contained readable mesh data and embedded thumbnails. Their calculated bounding dimensions were also checked to rule out a metre-to-millimetre scaling error.

### Interface Corrections and Confirmations

The latest interface information was consolidated into the test documentation:

- The dedicated USB-C interface uses a 9.60 x 3.80 mm opening with R1.80 corners.
- The separate ESP32 USB opening uses a 10 x 5 mm opening with R1 corners.
- The amplifier guide remains open on one side, and the omitted central tabs do not overlap the board.
- The speaker cradle consists of a 10 mm full circular section plus two additional 8 mm semicircular sections.
- The speaker-cradle side distances were updated to 19.0 mm and 16.5 mm.

### Physical Fit-Test Plan

A complete physical fit-test plan was produced as a PDF. It includes:

- General inspection and safety conditions
- Incoming print inspection
- Individual procedures for all eight coupon designs
- Pass, tight, loose, fail, and print-defect classifications
- Measurement and observation tables
- CAD revision rules
- Supplier print records and final sign-off fields

The plan treats the coupons as validation tools. It does not claim that the physical tests have already passed.

### Supplier Package

A supplier-ready ZIP archive was assembled with:

- Eight verified 3MF model files
- Physical Fit-Test Plan
- Supplier Print Brief
- File manifest with dimensions, byte sizes, and SHA-256 checksums
- README instructions

An English edition and a complete Brazilian Portuguese edition were prepared. In the Portuguese package, folders, filenames, the README, the manifest, and both PDFs were translated. The underlying 3MF geometry remained byte-for-byte unchanged.

### Supplier Research and Quotations

Brazilian 3D-printing services were researched for PETG prototyping and delivery to Goiás. The principal candidates were:

- Koios 3D
- 3D Create Goiânia
- ImaginaTech
- fábrica3D
- TRIDEO

A standardized Portuguese quotation-request message was prepared. The Portuguese supplier ZIP and the same technical requirements were used so that quotations could be compared consistently.

The Koios automatic quotation recognized eleven physical pieces. The initial configuration was corrected from 50% to 20% infill while retaining PETG and a 0.20 mm layer height. Three perimeters, no finishing, one lot, 1:1 scale, and supplier-generated G-code were established as the intended requirements.

### First Physical Component Received

The ESP32 development board was received. Precise measurements were deferred because a digital caliper was not yet available.

## Engineering Decisions

- Do not export or supply G-code before the selected supplier confirms the production printer and slicing profile.
- Use PETG, 0.20 mm layers, 20% infill, and three perimeters as the provisional common configuration for the fit coupons.
- Request no sanding, enlargement, repair, or geometric modification of fit-critical features.
- Obtain and compare multiple written quotations before selecting a supplier or authorizing production.
- Preserve the editable Fusion files as the design source and use 3MF files as the supplier geometry.

## Current Status

The fit-test coupon geometry, physical test plan, and supplier package are complete. Supplier responses and physical coupon production remain pending. No coupon has yet been physically tested, and the final enclosure remains subject to tolerance revisions based on those results.
