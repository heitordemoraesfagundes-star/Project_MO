# DL-010 — Replacement of the Original Mesh-Based Enclosure with a New Parametric Model

**Date:** 2026-08-24
**Status:** Accepted

---

## 1. Decision

Project MO will replace the original mesh-based enclosure as the primary production model with a **new simplified parametric enclosure created directly in Fusion 360**.

The original STL files will remain available as dimensional, visual, and stylistic references, but they will not be used as the editable foundation of the final V1 enclosure.

The new enclosure will be designed around the confirmed V1 hardware, beginning with the Nextion NX8048T050 display, ESP32, MAX98357A amplifier, 40 mm speaker, physical buttons, and D-pad. Its dimensions, wall thicknesses, clearances, openings, and mounting features will be controlled through Fusion 360 user parameters.

This decision supersedes the enclosure strategy recorded in DL-009.

---

## 2. Context

DL-009 established a fit-first and selective remodeling strategy in which the original STL enclosure would be retained whenever possible and only incompatible parts would be replaced.

Simplified component envelopes were subsequently created and positioned inside the original enclosure. Although the initial analysis indicated that most electronic components could fit, the selected display introduced a major compatibility constraint. The original creator used a Raspberry Pi DSI display, while Project MO requires a display compatible with the ESP32. Suitable alternatives either required additional controller hardware or exceeded the available dimensions of the original enclosure.

The original STL files also continued to create technical limitations in Fusion 360. Mesh repair and conversion produced fragmented bodies, incomplete surfaces, distorted rounded corners, and geometry that was difficult to inspect or modify accurately. These problems reduced confidence in collision checks and made even small revisions unnecessarily time-consuming.

A clean parametric model was therefore created around the selected V1 hardware. Initial modeling confirmed that a 150 mm × 200 mm × 65 mm enclosure can accommodate the Nextion display and its required clearance while providing additional space for the remaining components.

---

## 3. Alternatives Considered

| Option | Advantages | Disadvantages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Create a new simplified parametric enclosure** | Clean and editable geometry; reliable dimensions and interference checks; easy hardware-driven revisions; supports future expansion | Requires the enclosure and its mounting features to be modeled from the beginning |
| **Continue the selective remodeling strategy from DL-009** | Preserves more of the original enclosure; potentially reduces modeling work if few changes are required | Retains dependence on damaged mesh geometry; display integration and later modifications remain difficult |
| **Convert and repair all original STL files** | Attempts to preserve the original geometry in an editable form | Conversion produces fragmented or distorted solids; requires substantial repair time with uncertain results |
| **Use a different display to preserve the original enclosure** | Could reduce enclosure modifications | May increase electrical complexity, require additional controller hardware, or weaken ESP32 compatibility |
| **Use the original enclosure without modification** | Requires the least immediate CAD work | Does not provide sufficient confidence in display compatibility, mounting, clearances, or assembly |

---

## 4. Rationale

The new parametric enclosure was selected because the enclosure should be controlled by the confirmed functional requirements of Project MO rather than by the limitations of an imported mesh.

Creating the model directly in Fusion 360 provides reliable solid geometry, accurate measurements, editable dimensions, and repeatable interference checks. Master parameters allow the body size, wall thickness, corner radius, faceplate thickness, component clearances, and openings to be revised without rebuilding the complete model.

This strategy also permits the enclosure to be designed specifically around the Nextion NX8048T050 while preserving the ESP32 and MAX98357A architecture selected for V1. The original model will still guide the general proportions, rounded form, face layout, and visual identity of Project MO, but the new enclosure will remain mechanically independent from the original STL files.

The decision supports the Project MO priorities of simplicity, accessibility, modularity, reliable assembly, and efficient iteration.

---

## 5. Consequences

### Positive

* Produces clean, fully editable, and parametric CAD geometry.
* Enables reliable measurements, section views, and interference checks.
* Allows the enclosure to be sized around the confirmed V1 components.
* Simplifies changes to the display opening, controls, mounting points, and cable paths.
* Removes dependence on damaged or difficult-to-edit STL meshes.
* Supports separate files for the main body, faceplate, display envelope, and final assembly.
* Makes future hardware revisions easier to accommodate.
* Preserves the original model as a useful visual and dimensional reference.

### Trade-offs

* The main enclosure, faceplate, openings, mounts, and attachment features must be modeled from the beginning.
* The new enclosure may not reproduce every small detail of the original design.
* Additional verification will be required before printing, including wall thickness, assembly clearance, fastening, and printability checks.
* Existing fit analysis based on the original enclosure may need to be repeated using the new model.
* The project schedule must allocate time for CAD development before physical prototyping.
