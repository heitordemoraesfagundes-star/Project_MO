# DL-002 — System Architecture

**Date:** 2026-08-01  
**Status:** Accepted

---

## 1. Decision

Project MO will use a modular five-subsystem architecture consisting of Processing & Control, Power Management, Audio, User Interaction, and Mechanical Structure.

---

## 2. Context

As the project developed, MO began to involve several different functions, including computing, power distribution, audio output, user interaction, and physical construction.

A clear subsystem structure was therefore needed to prevent the design from becoming a single interconnected system and to make development, troubleshooting, and future expansion easier.

---

## 3. Alternatives Considered

| Option | Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|---|
| **Single Integrated System** | [Simple design ] | [Difficult to modify] |
| **Five Subsystem Architecture** | [Clear and supports modularity] | [Requires additional planning] |
| **Highly Granular Architecture** | [Maximum separation] | [Adds unecessary complexity] |

---

## 4. Rationale

The five-subsystem architecture was selected because it provides a balance between modularity and simplicity.

---

## 5. Consequences

**Benefits:** Clear separation between parts and easier troubleshooting and modifying

**Trade-offs:** Requires interfaces between subsystems to be explicitly defined
