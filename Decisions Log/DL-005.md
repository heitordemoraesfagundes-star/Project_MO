# DL-005 — Audio System Design

**Date:** 2026-08-04
**Status:** Accepted

---

## 1. Decision

Project MO V1 will use a **dedicated audio output subsystem consisting of the ESP32, an audio amplifier, and a speaker**.

The ESP32 will control the audio system, while the amplifier will provide the necessary power to drive the speaker.

---

## 2. Context

Audio is an important part of MO's interaction and personality. The system therefore requires a dedicated method of producing intelligible and sufficiently loud audio without placing unnecessary demands on the ESP32's output capabilities.

The audio system also needed to remain compact, affordable, and compatible with the project's overall power and hardware architecture.

---

## 3. Alternatives Considered

| Option                                    | Advantages                                                             | Disadvantages                                                |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **ESP32 + dedicated amplifier + speaker** | Better audio output; separates processing from amplification; scalable | Requires additional hardware and wiring                      |
| **ESP32 directly driving speaker**        | Simple and inexpensive                                                 | Insufficient output power and poor suitability for a speaker |
| **Larger external audio system**          | Greater audio capability                                               | Increased cost, size, power consumption, and complexity      |

---

## 4. Rationale

A dedicated amplifier was selected because it allows the ESP32 to focus on **audio processing and control** while the amplifier handles the electrical power required by the speaker.

This approach provides a better balance between audio quality, system complexity, cost, and physical size than the alternatives considered.

---

## 5. Consequences

### Positive

* Provides a dedicated path for audio output.
* Reduces the electrical demands placed on the ESP32.
* Allows the speaker to produce a more suitable output level.
* Keeps the audio subsystem modular and replaceable.

### Trade-offs

* Requires additional components and wiring.
* Increases power consumption compared with a minimal audio solution.
* The final audio quality is dependent on the selected amplifier and speaker.
