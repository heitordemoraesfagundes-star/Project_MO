# DL-004 — Interaction & Personality System

**Date:** 2026-08-03
**Status:** Accepted

---

## 1. Decision

Project MO V1 will prioritize **stationary interaction and personality** rather than physical movement.

MO will interact with users through its electronic inputs and outputs, with software behavior and audio serving as the primary mechanisms for expressing its personality.

---

## 2. Context

The original inspiration for Project MO was a small character-like robot, which suggested the possibility of creating a robot that feels interactive and expressive rather than simply functioning as a collection of electronic components.

During the definition of V1, physical movement was considered but ultimately determined to be unnecessary for achieving the project's primary goal: creating an engaging robot with a recognizable personality while maintaining a realistic development scope.

---

## 3. Alternatives Considered

| Option                           | Advantages                                                                                                    | Disadvantages                                                           |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Stationary interactive robot** | Simpler mechanical design; lower cost; easier to develop; allows greater focus on interaction and personality | Limited physical interaction                                            |
| **Moving robot**                 | More physically engaging; greater range of possible behaviors                                                 | Significantly increases mechanical, electrical, and software complexity |
| **Minimal interaction**          | Simplest implementation                                                                                       | Does not achieve the intended personality-driven experience             |

---

## 4. Rationale

The stationary approach was selected because it allows Project MO to focus its resources on **interaction, personality, and engineering quality** rather than introducing the additional complexity required for locomotion.

This decision also keeps V1 achievable within the project's timeline while leaving movement as a possible future expansion.

---

## 5. Consequences

### Positive

* Significantly reduces mechanical and electrical complexity.
* Allows more development effort to be dedicated to interaction and personality.
* Makes V1 more achievable within the planned development timeline.
* Provides a clear foundation for future versions.

### Trade-offs

* MO will not have physical locomotion in V1.
* Interaction is primarily limited to electronic and software-based behaviors.
