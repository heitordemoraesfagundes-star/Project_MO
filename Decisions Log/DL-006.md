# DL-006 — Power System Design

**Date:** 2026-08-17
**Status:** Accepted

---

## 1. Decision

Project MO V1 will use a centralized power system designed to distribute power to the major subsystems while providing basic electrical protection.

The power architecture will include appropriate voltage regulation/distribution and a **resettable fuse** to protect the system against excessive current.

---

## 2. Context

MO contains multiple electronic components with different power requirements, including the ESP32, audio system, and user-interaction hardware.

A power architecture was therefore needed that could provide stable power to the individual subsystems while minimizing the risk of component damage and keeping the system practical for V1.

---

## 3. Alternatives Considered

| Option                                                      | Advantages                                                          | Disadvantages                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Centralized power distribution with protection**          | Organized; easier to troubleshoot; provides system-level protection | Requires additional planning and components   |
| **Independent power sources for subsystems**                | Greater electrical isolation                                        | Increases size, cost, wiring, and maintenance |
| **Minimal power distribution without dedicated protection** | Simpler and cheaper                                                 | Greater risk of damage from electrical faults |

---

## 4. Rationale

A centralized power architecture was selected because it provides a **clear and manageable method of supplying the project's subsystems** while allowing protection components to be incorporated into the system.

A resettable fuse was selected as an additional protective measure because it can interrupt excessive current and recover after the fault is removed, avoiding the need to replace a conventional fuse.

---

## 5. Consequences

### Positive

* Provides a structured power distribution system.
* Reduces unnecessary wiring and separate power sources.
* Adds protection against excessive current.
* Makes the power system easier to troubleshoot and modify.
* Supports future expansion of the hardware.

### Trade-offs

* Adds additional components and design considerations.
* Requires careful attention to voltage and current requirements for each subsystem.
* The protection system does not replace proper electrical design and testing.
