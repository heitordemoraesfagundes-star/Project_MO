## Work Completed

### Preliminary ESP32 Measurements

The received ESP32 was measured with a normal ruler. Its length and width appeared consistent with the existing simplified model, but its complete assembly height differed from the original representation.

The preliminary observations were:

- PCB thickness of approximately 1 to 2 mm
- Pin projection of approximately 8 mm
- Total assembly thickness of approximately 10 mm
- Micro-USB connector approximately 7 mm wide and 2 mm high
- Connector body extending approximately 5 mm over the PCB
- Four corner mounting holes with an estimated diameter of 3 mm

These measurements are approximate and were not treated as final dimensional evidence.

### ESP32 CAD Assessment

The PCB outline remains likely to fit the existing 51.4 x 27.9 mm clearance, and the existing 10 x 5 mm ESP32 USB opening is still considered appropriately oversized for cable and print clearance.

The principal new risk is the approximately 8 mm underside pin projection. This may conflict with the enclosure floor because the simplified CAD model represented the PCB thickness but not the full pin-header envelope. The four corner holes could support a future standoff-based mounting revision, but their diameter and centre locations require more precise measurement.

No CAD change was made because ruler measurements may contain enough error to produce an incorrect revision. A digital caliper and the printed ESP32 coupon will be used before changing the mount.

### Supplier Response Review

3D Create Goiânia reviewed the model set and correctly observed that eight files produced eleven printable objects. This was confirmed as expected because the limb-interface coupon contains four separate pieces.

The supplier reported:

- Bambu Lab A1 Combo with AMS capability
- Availability of a standard 0.4 mm nozzle and a 0.2 mm nozzle
- PETG from multiple brands
- Estimated production time of one day when the machine is available
- PIX and credit-card payment options

The 0.4 mm nozzle was retained as the preferred choice. A 0.2 mm nozzle would increase printing time without providing meaningful value for these functional fit coupons.

The supplier was asked to use:

- PETG, preferably grey
- 0.4 mm nozzle
- 0.20 mm layer height
- 20% infill
- Three perimeters
- One complete lot
- No sanding or dimensional adjustment of fit features

It was also clarified that the Fusion-generated 3MF files primarily contain geometry and that the supplier must confirm the requested slicing settings rather than assume they are embedded in the files.

### Micro-USB Cable Test

The ESP32 was connected to a Windows computer to determine whether the available micro-USB cable supported data transfer.

The Windows Device Manager was changed to the **Devices by type** view, and the system was checked for:

- A new COM port
- CP210x or CH340 USB-to-serial hardware
- An unknown USB device
- A device with a driver-warning symbol

No device appeared and Windows produced no clear detection result. Because no second cable known to support data transfer was available, the test remained inconclusive. The cable may be charge-only, but a missing USB-to-serial driver or hardware issue cannot yet be excluded.

## Engineering Decisions

- Do not modify the ESP32 mount based only on ruler measurements.
- Keep the current 10 x 5 mm ESP32 USB opening until a physical test indicates otherwise.
- Prioritize precise measurement of the pin projection, connector location, and mounting-hole centres.
- Use a 0.4 mm nozzle for the fit coupons.
- Treat the micro-USB cable test as inconclusive until a known data cable or another compatible device is available.
- Keep supplier production pending until the technical configuration, total price, delivery time, and payment are reviewed.

## Current Risks and Open Questions

- The ESP32 pins may require greater clearance above the enclosure floor.
- The four mounting holes cannot be used reliably until their diameter and spacing are measured precisely.
- The available micro-USB cable may not support data transfer.
- Supplier quotations and final technical confirmations are still pending.
- No physical coupon results are available yet.
